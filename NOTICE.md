# Notice on third-party material

The MIT license in `LICENSE.txt` covers only the material created in this project: source code, documentation and scripts.

The tests contain material from the 1988-1989 KOMPAS-based accounting systems written by Pavel Severov (NIS Gidroproekt):
- schemas, loader decks and MAD queries (`tests/gold-*`);
- application programs, converted to Free Pascal (`tests/severov`).

The project relies mostly on this material: it is the description of the languages the tools implement. Its inclusion does not mean that any rights to it passed to this project. The status of the rights has not been established.

If you hold rights to KOMPAS material and want to clarify the terms of its use, please open an issue in this repository.
